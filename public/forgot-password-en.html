Forgot Password

Follow the steps below to reset your password from the app.

📱

Password reset is done exclusively via the Diaspo Niger mobile app.

How to reset your password

1

Open the Diaspo Niger app on your mobile device.

2

On the login screen, tap "Forgot password?"

3

Enter your email address linked to your account.

4

Check your inbox and click the reset link we sent you.

5

Create a new secure password (at least 8 characters).

Can't access your email?

Contact our support team with your account details:

  • support@diasponiger.com
  • Include your full name and any information that helps identify your account
  • Our team will respond within 48 business hours

Security tips

  • Use a unique and complex password
  • Never share your password with anyone
  • Enable two-factor authentication if available
  • Change your password regularly
