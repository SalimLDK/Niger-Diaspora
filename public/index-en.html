Diplomatic posts

The post you depend on, and how to reach it

The directory of Niger's embassies, consulates and permanent missions, from Côte d'Ivoire to China.

  • Grouped by continent then by country, closest to you first.
  • Address, phone number, opening hours and location of every post.
  • Call or open directions straight from the list.
Twenty procedures

What to bring, before you make the trip

Every consular procedure is spelled out: the documents to bring, the steps, where it is done, and what the official source leaves unsaid.

  • Consular registration
  • Civil status records — birth, marriage, death, transcriptions
  • Travel documents — passport, laissez-passer, safe conduct
  • Notarised deeds and certificates
  • Nationality
The file

The documents to gather

The consular card is the way into everything else: eighteen of the twenty procedures ask for it first. Without a Nigerien identity document, two witnesses who are already registered will do — it is written in the official text, and the app says so too.

When the source does not publish a fee, the app says so rather than inventing a number.

Consular registration
Consular card
  • Copy of a Nigerien identity document — certificate of nationality, birth certificate or previous consular card

or, failing that

  • Two Nigerien witnesses who already hold the consular card
  • Two recent passport photographs

Chancellery fees: amount not published by the source.

Approximate location

Who, near you, comes from the same country

The map shows members around you. It works both ways, and you decide whether you appear on it.

  • Approximate position, never your exact address.
  • Switch it off at any time from your profile.
  • Invisible to accounts you have blocked.
  • Without location, the map is browsed city by city.
End-to-end encrypted

Groups by city, conversations that stay between you

Join the group for your city or your host country, or start your own. Messages are end-to-end encrypted.

  • Text, photos, videos, voice messages and GIFs.
  • Audio and video calls, one to one or in a group.
  • Polls and pinned messages inside groups.
  • Official groups per country, open to every member.

Our mission

Born elsewhere, Nigerien for life

Diaspo Niger is a mobile app built to connect Nigeriens living around the world. The goal is simple: nobody should have to work out alone which embassy they depend on, what papers a procedure needs, or where other Nigeriens in their city are.

You stay in charge of what you show: location can be switched off, the profile fills in at your own pace, and conversations are end-to-end encrypted.
