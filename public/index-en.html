A community without borders

However far apart.
The community stays close.

From Niamey to Montreal, from Paris to Abidjan, find the members of the Nigerien community around you — and keep in touch with those who stayed.

Your location stays under your control: it is approximate, never your exact address, and can be switched off at any time.

The app

All of Diaspo Niger in your pocket.

One community, several ways to connect. Pick one, the screen follows.

Communities

Find your people.

Groups form the way the diaspora lives: by city, by host country, or around whatever brings you together.

By city

The group for the city you live in: good tips, meetups, and the people who have just arrived and are finding their bearings.

By host country

An official group in every country, open to all members, for what concerns everyone, in one place.

By interest

Culture, studies, business: create the group that is missing, invite people, and keep it alive with polls and pinned messages.
