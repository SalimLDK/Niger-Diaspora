Our mission

Born elsewhere, Nigerien forever

Diaspo Niger is a mobile platform designed to connect Nigeriens living around the world. Our goal is to build a supportive and dynamic community where everyone can share, exchange, and help one another.

Whether you're looking to reconnect with compatriots in your city, attend cultural events, or simply stay connected with your roots, Diaspo Niger is the space for you.
