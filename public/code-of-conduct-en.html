Our Vision

A welcoming space for everyone

Diaspo Niger is a community platform designed to connect Nigeriens around the world. Our mission is to create a welcoming, respectful and inclusive space where every member can:

  • Connect with their community
  • Exchange freely and respectfully
  • Share their culture and traditions
  • Develop professional opportunities
  • Maintain ties with their home country
Our Values

The foundations of our community

Mutunci
Respect

Treating every member with dignity, regardless of their origin, region, status or opinions.

Taimako
Solidarity

Mutual aid and support within the diaspora, in the spirit of the Nigerien community.

Gaskiya
Authenticity

Being genuine and sincere in interactions, without deception or manipulation.

Bambancin
Diversity

Celebrating the richness of our regional, ethnic and cultural differences.

Nauyi
Responsibility

Taking responsibility for one's words and actions on the platform.

Expected Behaviours

How to behave well

In Conversations and Messages

Behaviour Example
Respectful communication Use polite and constructive language
Active listening Consider different points of view
Constructive debate Argue about ideas, not people
Kindness Welcome new members
Mutual help Answer questions and share knowledge

In Groups and Communities

  • Contribute positively to discussions
  • Respect the specific rules of each group
  • Help maintain a healthy environment
  • Include all members, even less active ones
Prohibited Behaviours

What is not tolerated

🚫 Zero Tolerance — Immediate Ban
  • Child exploitation: Any content or behaviour involving minors in an inappropriate manner
  • Terrorism: Glorification, promotion or organisation of terrorist acts
  • Human trafficking: Organisation or promotion of trafficking
  • Sale of illegal substances: Drugs, diverted medications
  • Death threats: Direct threats against a person's life

Harassment and Intimidation

  • Psychological harassment: repeated unsolicited messages, psychological pressure
  • Sexual harassment: unwanted advances, sexual comments
  • Cyberbullying: smear campaigns, spreading rumours
  • Intimidation: threats, blackmail, pressure
  • Doxxing: revealing personal information without consent

Discrimination and Hatred

  • Ethnic discrimination: insults or mockery based on ethnicity or region
  • Religious discrimination: attacks on religious beliefs
  • Gender discrimination: sexism, misogyny, homophobia
  • Xenophobia: rejection or insults towards other nationalities
  • Hate speech: statements aimed at dehumanising a group

Fraud and Deception

  • Scams: false promises to obtain money
  • Identity theft: impersonating someone else
  • Con schemes: fraudulent sales, fake services
  • Manipulation: false information to manipulate members
  • Phishing: attempts to steal credentials

Inappropriate Content

  • Unsolicited pornography
  • Extreme graphic violence
  • Gore: content showing serious injuries
  • Deliberate disinformation
  • Spam and unsolicited advertising
Ceremony Rooms

Sacred spaces

🕌

These spaces deserve particular respect — they accompany the most important moments of our lives.

Type Expected behaviour
Weddings Sincere congratulations, shared joy, respect for privacy
Naming ceremonies Kindness towards new parents
Bereavements Respectful condolences, support, no conflicts
Religious ceremonies Respect for practices, no proselytising

Strictly prohibited in these spaces

  • Inappropriate or disrespectful comments
  • Family conflicts aired publicly
  • Unsolicited requests for money
  • Unauthorised recordings
Moderation

Sanction Scale

Level Sanction Duration Examples
1 Warning — First minor offence
2 Temporary restriction 24–72h Repeat offence, inappropriate remarks
3 Short suspension 7–14 days Mild harassment, minor fraud
4 Long suspension 30–90 days Serious harassment, repeated offences
5 Ban Permanent Serious violations, multiple repeat offences
Reporting

Reporting and Appeals

How to report

📣

In the app: "Report" button on each piece of content/profile
By email: moderation@diasponiger.com
Form: Settings › Help › Report a problem

Processing times

Type of report Deadline
Emergencies (threats, child exploitation) < 1 hour
Harassment, fraud < 24 hours
Inappropriate content < 48 hours
Other reports < 7 days

Right of appeal

If you contest a sanction:

  1. Deadline: 15 days after notification
  2. Email: support@diasponiger.com
  3. Content: Detailed explanation, any evidence
  4. Response time: 15 working days
